# Wyrmspan player mat (read from Justin's photo, Nov 15)
Explore path per cave (adventurer walks left to right; stops at the first space without a dragon, or at the end):
- Crimson Cavern: entrance any resource · after col 1 guild +1 · after col 2 any resource · after col 3 lay 1 egg · end: cache up to 2 resources from your supply onto any dragon(s)
- Golden Grotto: entrance dragon card · after col 1 guild +1 · after col 2 dragon card · after col 3 lay 1 egg · end: tuck up to 2 dragon cards from your hand under any dragon(s)
- Amethyst Abyss: entrance cave card · after col 1 guild +1 · after col 2 cave card · after col 3 lay 1 egg · end: discard up to 2 cave cards from your hand, lay 1 egg for each
Costs: excavate 1 coin (+1 egg col 3, +2 eggs col 4); entice 1 coin + printed cost; explore the same cave 1st/2nd/3rd time a round: 1 coin / +1 egg / +2 eggs.
Column 4 "when covered" (once, when you excavate it): may spend 3 items (resources, dragon cards, cave cards from hand, any mix) for 1 coin. Optional; full price even on free excavations.
Left panel: turn end discard to 9 cards (dragon+cave), 9 coins, 9 resources; any time trade 2 resources for 1; the mat stores 2 eggs.
Round tracker: R1 4/1/0/0, R2 5/2/1/0, R3 6/3/2/0, R4 7/4/3/0 (1st/2nd/3rd/others); ties take the higher place, the next player the next place; 0 items = others.
Objective tile numbers are the solo Automa's counts, not thresholds.
