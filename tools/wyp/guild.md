# Wyrmspan Dragon Guild (read from Space-Biff + Polygon photos, Nov 15)
Track: 12 spaces, clockwise. Advancing 1 = move 1 space and gain that space.
0 START (brown): place an unused marker on the guild tile below (an ability row or end-game row)
1 egg (lay) · 2 meat · 3 dragon card · 4 cave card · 5 crystal
6 BOTTOM (brown): place an unused marker on the tile above
7 egg · 8 gold · 9 dragon card · 10 coin · 11 milk
Tile (one per game, side by player count): 3 ability rows + guild end-game row + "END GAME gain printed VP" row (6, 3, then 1 each unlimited).
Marker boxes per row (ability 1 / 2 / 3 / guild end-game), from Justin's sheet:
  Rainforest 2–3p 1/1/1/2 · 4–5p 2/2/2/3
  Highlands  2–3p 1/2/1/1 · 4–5p 2/3/2/2
  Seafarers  2–3p 1/1/2/1 · 4–5p 2/2/3/2
  Plains     2–3p 2/1/1/1 · 4–5p 3/2/2/2
  The 6/3/1 'printed VP' row is not limited. Max 4 markers per player.
Rainforest: pay 1 any resource → excavate 1 free (cave card from hand or display) · play a dragon from hand or display ignoring all resource costs · gain 1 coin + 2 any resources · END 1 VP per resource cost icon on dragons in 1 of your caves
Highlands: pay 1 coin → excavate up to 2 free with cave cards from hand · pay 1 cave card → play a dragon from hand on top of any dragon on your mat, free · gain 1 coin + 2 cave cards · END 1 VP per cave card on your mat (incl. covered)
Plains (costs confirmed in English): pay 1 egg → draw 1 cave card from deck and play it free · pay 2 eggs → play 1 dragon from hand free · gain 1 coin + lay 2 eggs · END 1 VP per 2 eggs (rounded down)
Seafarers (Justin's photo, 4–5p side, boxes 2/2/3/2): excavate 1 free with a cave card from hand · pay 1 dragon card → play a dragon from hand free · gain 1 coin + 2 dragon cards · END 3 VP per filled column of dragons on your mat

Track order confirmed by Justin's Seafarers photo (the black wing-shell icon is the coin).
