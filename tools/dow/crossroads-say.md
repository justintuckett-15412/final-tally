# Dead of Winter crossroads: the app's wording (check against your cards)

Our own short wording of each card's trigger and options (mechanics only; the story is on the card). 👍/👎 = vote cards (thumbs up = first option).

**1. Car Crash**
- When: You move a survivor using a fuel card
- Option 1: That survivor gets frostbite and is placed back at the colony
- Option 2: That survivor arrives, but rolls for exposure 3 times

**2. Wavering Loyalties**
- When: You have more survivors than at least one other player
- Option 1: Give one of your followers to a player with fewer survivors
- Option 2: Roll a die: on 1–3 lose 1 morale

**3. Horde**
- When: You move a survivor without using fuel
- Option 1: Roll for exposure 3 more times on that survivor
- Option 2: That survivor goes back where it came from and cannot move again this turn

**4. Addiction**
- When: You play a medicine card to heal a survivor
- Option 1: The healed survivor dies (no morale loss)
- Option 2: The healed survivor is hooked: at the start of each of your turns, play a medicine on it or lose an unused die

**5. Spoiled**
- When: You spend food tokens to raise a die
- Option 1: Put a wound on one of your survivors; the die still goes up
- Option 2: Throw it out: the food is spent but the die does not go up

**6. Maude**
- When: One of your survivors is at the grocery store
- Option 1: That survivor goes back to the colony and you add 3 food
- Option 2: Take 2 food cards from the grocery deck; you cannot play food cards this turn

**7. Sparky**
- When: You control Sparky
- Option 1: Kill 8 zombies at the colony, no exposure rolls
- Option 2: Gain 1 morale

**8. Maria Lopez**
- When: You control Maria Lopez
- Option 1: Pay 3 food tokens: take any 3 cards from the school deck
- Option 2: Remove all food cards from the school deck

**9. Brian Lee**
- When: You control Brian Lee and he is at the colony
- Option 1: Clear all zombies from the hospital and build 4 barricades there
- Option 2: Clear all zombies from the grocery store and build 4 barricades there

**10. Lover's Quarrel**
- When: Two of your (human) survivors share a location away from the colony
- Option 1: Add 4 noise tokens where they are
- Option 2: Those two cannot attack, search or move for the rest of this turn

**11. Mugging**
- When: Your survivor shares a location with an exiled survivor
- Option 1: Give all but 1 card in your hand to the exiled survivor's player
- Option 2: Fight back: 2 wounds on your survivor there (with a weapon you may kill the exiled survivor and take only 1)

**12. Hunger**
- When: You add a male survivor and a helpless survivor to the colony
- Option 1: All the food in the supply is eaten
- Option 2: Add 5 food and lose 1 morale

**13. Thin Ice**
- When: You move a survivor
- Option 1: Roll a die: 1–3 the moved survivor gets frostbite; 4–6 take any card from any item deck
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**14. Helpless** (vote)
- When: One of your survivors is at the colony (vote)
- 👍 Remove all helpless survivors; lose 1 morale per 2 removed (rounded up)
- 👎 Nothing happens

**15. Christmas** (vote)
- When: One of your survivors is at the colony (vote)
- 👍 Spend 5 food, gain 1 morale; Forest Plum's player may hand out up to 4 grocery cards to other players
- 👎 Nothing happens

**16. Talia Jones**
- When: You control Talia Jones
- Option 1: Pick an item deck and take any 4 cards from it
- Option 2: The next crisis revealed is removed and that round has no crisis

**17. Arthur Thurston**
- When: You control Arthur Thurston
- Option 1: Put 2 wounds on Arthur
- Option 2: Lose 1 morale

**18. Gray Beard**
- When: You move a survivor while Gray Beard is still in the survivor deck
- Option 1: Remove a random card in your hand from the game
- Option 2: Gray Beard becomes your group leader: once per round he attacks without exposure, but while you control him you can't add cards to crises

**19. Sophie Robinson**
- When: You move a survivor while Sophie Robinson is still in the survivor deck
- Option 1: Sophie joins your following with 2 wounds
- Option 2: Draw 2 cards from an item deck of your choice; Sophie leaves the game

**20. Wounded Survivor**
- When: You search with a survivor
- Option 1: A new survivor joins your following; roll for exposure twice on them
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**21. Creature Comforts**
- When: One of your survivors is at the colony
- Option 1: Players may together put 3 fuel cards in the waste pile (no effect) to gain 1 morale

**22. Outbreak**
- When: Your survivor is at the colony with at least 1 helpless survivor
- Option 1: Send a helpless survivor away; lose 1 morale
- Option 2: Every survivor at the colony gets 1 wound; unless Olivia Brown or Janet Taylor is in play, each helpless survivor dies on a roll of 1

**23. Olivia Brown** (vote)
- When: Olivia Brown is in play (she goes to the colony, then vote)
- 👍 Olivia experiments each round at the colony: roll exposure on her; on a blank, medicine can cancel bites that round
- 👎 Nothing happens

**24. Brandon Kane**
- When: Brandon Kane is in play
- Option 1: Put 1 wound on Brandon and place him at the colony
- Option 2: Put the last 10 item cards removed from the game into the waste pile

**25. No Ammo**
- When: You use a weapon card’s ability
- Option 1: That weapon goes to the waste pile
- Option 2: All your unused dice become used

**26. Forest Plum**
- When: Forest Plum is in play
- Option 1: Forest goes to the grocery store; add 4 noise tokens there
- Option 2: The survivor with the lowest influence (not Forest) gets 1 wound

**27. Daniel Smith**
- When: You control Daniel Smith and there is a helpless survivor (one is removed)
- Option 1: Spend a die: jail a survivor at the police station; their items go back to its owner's hand and it can't move, search, attack or use its ability again
- Option 2: Lose 1 morale

**28. Little Survivors**
- When: You move a survivor to a location with no other survivors
- Option 1: Add 3 helpless survivors and gain 1 morale
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**29. Prophet**
- When: Your exiled survivor shares a location with a non-exiled survivor
- Option 1: Take a non-exiled survivor at a location with yours into your following
- Option 2: Lose 1 morale

**30. Music**
- When: You search with a survivor
- Option 1: Gain 1 morale
- Option 2: Every player rolls 1 extra action die next round

**31. Collapsing Barriers** (vote)
- When: Your survivor is at the colony, which has a barricade and a zombie (vote)
- 👍 Each player with survivors at the colony rolls exposure on one of them
- 👎 Remove all barricades at the colony

**32. Radio Signal** (vote)
- When: One of your survivors is at the colony (vote)
- 👍 Players may together give up 3 unused dice: then add 1 helpless survivor and gain 1 morale; if not, lose 1 morale
- 👎 Lose 1 morale

**33. Toxic Waste** (vote)
- When: One of your survivors is at the colony (vote)
- 👍 Each player puts frostbite on one of their survivors at the colony
- 👎 Put every removed fuel and medicine card into the waste pile

**34. Alexis Grey**
- When: You control Alexis Grey (she goes to the library if there is room)
- Option 1: Alexis can never leave the library
- Option 2: Alexis goes to the colony and can never go to the library

**35. James Meyers**
- When: You control James Meyers
- Option 1: See every player's hand; you may remove 3 medicine cards from among them to gain 1 morale
- Option 2: See one player's secret objective

**36. Bev Russell**
- When: You control Bev Russell (she goes to the school if there is room)
- Option 1: Bev guards the school for good (her card leaves the game); any survivor moving to the school takes 1 wound
- Option 2: Remove all item cards and zombies from the school

**37. The Deputy**
- When: One of your survivors is away from the colony
- Option 1: Take every weapon among the top 8 police station cards; the main objective now also needs no zombies at the gas station, hospital and police station
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**38. Sewer Passage**
- When: One of your survivors is at the library
- Option 1: Roll a die and roll exposure that many times on your survivor there; if it lives, moves between the colony and the hospital never need exposure rolls
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**39. Horse**
- When: You move a survivor
- Option 1: The moved survivor gets a horse: it never rolls exposure when moving, but the colony pays 1 extra food each round

**40. Thomas Heart** (Thomas Heart's player chooses)
- When: You use a weapon card's ability while Thomas Heart is in play (his player chooses)
- Option 1: Thomas spreads 4 wounds among the other survivors at his location
- Option 2: Players together remove 2 medicine cards in their hands from the game
- Option 3: Kill Thomas

**41. Rod Miller**
- When: Rod Miller is in play
- Option 1: Rod and 2 of your survivors go to the hospital and each rolls exposure twice (survivors there may be sent to the colony to make room)
- Option 2: Kill Rod

**42. Supply Drop**
- When: One of your survivors is at the colony
- Option 1: Roll exposure 3 times on one of your survivors; if it lives, add 5 food and gain 1 morale
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**43. Army** (vote)
- When: One of your survivors is at the colony (everyone with a survivor there votes)
- 👍 The main objective now also needs 5 food in the supply
- 👎 Roll a die for each survivor at the colony: on 1–3 it gets a wound
- Instead: if Thomas Heart is at the colony, nothing happens

**44. Gambling** (vote)
- When: One of your survivors is at the colony (vote)
- 👍 Each player may stake 1 card per survivor they have at the colony; the highest roll takes them all
- 👎 Nothing happens

**45. Bitter Cold Night** (vote)
- When: Your survivor gets frostbite moving to a location with other survivors (they vote)
- 👍 Every survivor there gets frostbite
- 👎 The moving survivor dies

**46. Lighthouse**
- When: One of your survivors is at the colony
- Option 1: You may give up 1 unused die to remove 1 zombie from every location

**47. Sacrament**
- When: You move a survivor
- Option 1: Roll exposure twice on the moved survivor; if it lives, gain 1 morale
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**48. Outsiders** (vote)
- When: One of your survivors is at the colony (vote)
- 👍 Each player takes a new survivor and adds 1 helpless survivor to the colony
- 👎 Nothing happens

**49. Riot**
- When: Your survivor is at the colony and helpless survivors outnumber the others there
- Option 1: All food in the supply is lost
- Option 2: Each non-helpless survivor at the colony gets 1 wound
- Option 3: Kill 1 helpless survivor

**50. The Sunshine State**
- When: Your survivor is at the colony with 2 or more helpless survivors
- Option 1: Remove 2 helpless survivors and lose 1 morale
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**51. Flier**
- When: One of your survivors is at the colony
- Only option: At the end of next round, lose 1 morale if any location has no barricade; gain 1 morale if every location has 2 or more

**52. Airplane** (vote)
- When: You move a survivor while Sophie Robinson is in play (vote)
- 👍 Players may together remove a tool card, a fuel card and Sophie from the game to gain 1 morale
- 👎 Nothing happens

**53. Missing Child** (vote)
- When: There is at least 1 helpless survivor at the colony (vote)
- 👍 If every location away from the colony is searched by the end of next round, gain 1 morale; if not, lose 1 morale and a helpless survivor
- 👎 Lose 1 morale and remove a helpless survivor

**54. Abandoned** (vote)
- When: You clean waste while there is a helpless survivor (vote)
- 👍 Add 1 helpless survivor and 5 zombies at the colony
- 👎 Add 5 zombies at the colony; each player gives up 1 unused die if they can

**55. Mike Cho**
- When: Your survivor is at a location with zombies while Mike Cho is in the survivor deck
- Option 1: Mike Cho joins you; 1 wound on him and on your survivor there; clear the zombies there
- Option 2: Clear all zombies from that location

**56. John Price**
- When: You search at the school while John Price is in the survivor deck
- Option 1: John Price joins you; add 2 zombies at the school
- Option 2: Add 2 food; John Price leaves the game

**57. Ashley Ross**
- When: You control Ashley Ross
- Option 1: Raised walkways: no exposure when attacking zombies at colony entrance 1; Ashley rolls exposure twice
- Option 2: Bigger waste area: lose 1 morale per 15 waste cards instead of 10; Ashley rolls exposure once
- Option 3: Nothing happens

**58. Edward White**
- When: You control Edward White
- Option 1: Pay 1 food token: take a removed medicine card into your hand
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**59. Heads Up**
- When: You search at the police station
- Option 1: Fill the police station with noise tokens

**60. Sleep**
- When: Another player yawns (the drawer taps “They yawned”)
- Only option: Until the round ends, each player may give up all their dice at the start of their turn to heal 1 wound on each of their survivors

**61. Buddy Davis**
- When: You control Buddy Davis and he starts your turn at the colony
- Option 1: Workouts: at the start of each round, any player may waste a food card to roll 1 extra die
- Option 2: Gain 1 morale

**62. Dynamite** (Edward White's player chooses)
- When: Edward White and John Price are both in play (Edward's player chooses)
- Option 1: Roll a die: on 2 or more, roll again and remove that many zombies from the colony
- Option 2: Everyone rolls 1 less die next round; gain 1 morale

**63. Gabriel Diaz**
- When: Your survivor is away from the colony while Gabriel Diaz is in the survivor deck
- Option 1: Gabriel joins your following; add 3 helpless survivors
- Option 2: Remove 1 card in your hand from the game

**64. Andrew Evans**
- When: You control Andrew Evans (he goes to the grocery store)
- Option 1: Greenhouse: Andrew gains “at the colony, once per round, spend a 1+ die to add 2 food”
- Option 2: Gain 1 morale

**65. Annaleigh Chan**
- When: You control Annaleigh Chan
- Option 1: Add 1 zombie at each location away from the colony
- Option 2: Kill Annaleigh

**66. David Garcia**
- When: You control David Garcia (he goes to the colony; you see 1 random card of each player there)
- Option 1: Accuse a player at the colony: start a vote to exile them
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**67. Crazed Survivor**
- When: One of your survivors is away from the colony
- Option 1: Add 3 food; roll exposure twice on one of your survivors away from the colony
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**68. Welcome Home**
- When: You move a survivor
- Option 1: Roll exposure once on the moved survivor; if it lives, once per round it attacks without exposure
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**69. Book of the Dead**
- When: You search at the library
- Option 1: Gain 1 morale
- Option 2: Remove 1 zombie at the library and add 4 food

**70. Rascals**
- When: You search at the hospital
- Option 1: Add 5 zombies at the colony
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**71. Janet Taylor**
- When: You control Janet Taylor
- Option 1: While Janet is at the colony, a player may waste a card from hand (no effect) to heal 1 wound on their survivor at the colony

**72. Old Divisions** (vote)
- When: One of your survivors is at the colony (vote)
- 👍 Remove 1 helpless survivor; this round’s crisis goes to the bottom of the deck unresolved; lose 1 morale
- 👎 Nothing happens

**73. Voyeur**
- When: Your survivor and another player's are alone together somewhere (you see their hand)
- Option 1: Take a card of your choice from their hand (it cannot be played until next round)
- Option 2: Show their hand to everyone

**74. The Arctic King**
- When: You search at the gas station
- Option 1: The searching survivor gets a snowmobile: no exposure when it moves, but it makes noise at both ends (a zombie at the colony instead) and wears out after 4 moves
- Option 2: Take 2 fuel cards from the gas station deck

**75. Carla Thompson**
- When: You control Carla Thompson (she goes to the police station)
- Option 1: Roll exposure twice on Carla; if she lives, gain 1 morale
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**76. Jenny Clark**
- When: You search with Jenny Clark
- Option 1: Jenny goes to the colony; add 8 zombies there

**77. Hunted**
- When: Your survivor gets a (non-frostbite) wound from exposure after moving
- Option 1: Roll exposure twice on each of your survivors
- Option 2: From now on every move also rolls a die: on 1–2 that survivor gets a wound

**78. Loretta Clay**
- When: You control Loretta Clay
- Option 1: Roll a die: 5–6 gain 1 morale; 3–4 nothing; 1–2 each survivor at the colony rolls, and on 1–2 gets a wound
- Option 2: Remove 1 food from the supply

**79. Fuel Tanker**
- When: You move a survivor
- Option 1: Add 6 zombies at the colony; take up to 4 removed fuel cards into your hand
- Option 2: Nothing happens

**80. Harman Brooks**
- When: Harman Brooks is in play
- Option 1: Add 3 food
- Option 2: Remove 1 wound from up to 3 survivors
