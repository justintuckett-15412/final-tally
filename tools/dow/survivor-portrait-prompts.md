# Dead of Winter survivor portraits: ChatGPT sheets

5 sheets of 6, all 30 survivors in A–Z order. For each sheet, paste the style block, then the sheet's list, in a new chat. Keep the order exactly: the app cuts each sheet into 6 pictures in order, left to right, top row first.

Each portrait is an original design from the survivor's name and job only. Don't copy the real card art.

## Style block (paste with every sheet)
A landscape image, 1536 × 1024, made of a 3 by 2 grid of six separate square pictures (3 across, 2 down), each one exactly one third of the width and half the height, separated by thin straight white lines. No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no borders, no frames. Each picture is a head-and-shoulders portrait of one original character, facing slightly to the side, against a soft snowy winter background, filling most of the square. Painted winter survival look: gentle shading, fine ink outlines, cold blue-grey tones with warm highlights, snow on their clothes. Not gory, no blood, no weapons pointed at the viewer. Each person looks clearly different from the others. No real people, no celebrities, no meeples.
