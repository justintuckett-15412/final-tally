### Notes and hedges

- **Florence Lawrence**: Wikipedia says she is "credited with" the signals and never patented them.
- **Mary Anning**: the "She sells seashells" link is disputed, so the card uses her ichthyosaur find instead.

- **Robert Chesebrough**: eating it daily was his own claim, and the card says "claimed".
- **Ellen Church**: a man (Heinrich Kubis) was a flight attendant earlier; the card says first *female* flight attendant.
- **Bill Bowerman**: also a Nike co-founder. The card is about the waffle iron, so the answer doesn't give that away.
